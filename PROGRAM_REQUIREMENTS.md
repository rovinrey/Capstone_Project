# 📋 TUPAD Program Application Guide

## 🛠 Basic Requirements
*Ensure you have both original copies and photocopies of the following:*

- [ ] **Government Issued ID** (Valid and with address)
- [ ] **Barangay Certification** (Stating you are a resident and a displaced/disadvantaged worker)
- [ ] **Application Form** (Provided by DOLE or your local Public Employment Service Office - PESO)
- [ ] **Birth Certificate** (PSA copy, if required by your local unit)

---

## ⚖️ Eligibility & Rules

### ⏳ The 6-Month Cooling Period
According to DOLE guidelines to ensure fair distribution of work:
> **Rule:** A beneficiary is only eligible to avail of TUPAD assistance **once every six (6) months**. 

* **Scenario:** If you just finished a TUPAD work cycle today, you must wait at least 6 months before you are eligible to be selected for the program again.
* **Purpose:** This prevents "double-dipping" and ensures other community members in need get a chance to participate.

### Other Key Rules:
1. **Age Limit:** Must be at least 18 years old. There is no upper age limit as long as the person is fit to work.
2. **One Per Household:** Generally, only one member per household is prioritized to give others a chance.
3. **Work Duration:** Usually spans 10 to 30 days depending on the project.
4. **Attire:** Beneficiaries must wear the provided TUPAD T-shirt and safety gear (PPE) during work hours.
